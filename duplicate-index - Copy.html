Our Story

Engineering the Infrastructure of Tomorrow's Africa

ADIPATS Consulting is a Nigerian enterprise software company with over two decades of proven excellence building mission-critical systems for the continent's most respected financial institutions, universities, and government bodies.

We don't build generic software. We architect bespoke, institution-grade platforms that handle billions in transactions, millions of students, and thousands of pension beneficiaries — reliably, securely, and at scale.

From cheque truncation systems for commercial banks to pension management platforms for retirement administrators, our solutions power the invisible infrastructure that keeps Africa's economy running.
